Office Productivity

Smarter collaboration starts here. Setup of office suites: Google Workspace and Microsoft 365.

Connect your workforce with secure productivity platforms that improve teamwork, streamline communication and keep your business running efficiently.

We set up your suite on your own domain, move your existing mail and files across, and make sure your team knows how to use it.

What you get

  • Professional email on your domain
  • Migration from existing mail and storage
  • User, group and permission setup
  • Security settings and two-factor authentication
  • Training and ongoing admin support

Google Workspace vs Microsoft 365

AspectGoogle WorkspaceMicrosoft 365
Core appsGmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive, MeetOutlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneDrive, Teams
Working styleBrowser-first, real-time collaborationStrong desktop apps plus web versions
Best fitTeams that live in the browser and share constantlyTeams that rely on advanced Excel/Word features
